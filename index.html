The Shop

Built for the neighborhood. Squared away for the military.

“As members of the local community, our goal is to support our neighbors and our military.”

Thunder 6 is a USMC combat veteran owned and operated, full-service barber shop in Mission Marketplace on College Boulevard. A full crew behind the chairs means regulars, first-timers and kids all get the same thing: a clean, consistent cut and a shop that takes pride in the work.
